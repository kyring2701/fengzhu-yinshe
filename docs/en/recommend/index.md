# 🎹 Recommend a Piece

Have a song you'd love to play? Submit your recommendations here and let's perform it together!

## 📝 How to Recommend

1. Click the link below to open the recommendation form.
2. Fill in the piece title, original artist, and genre/style.
3. Briefly describe why you want to perform this piece.
4. After submission, we'll arrange projects based on everyone's choices!

## 🎵 Recommendation Guidelines

- All styles welcome: ACG, wind music, orchestral, rock, jazz — everything is fine.
- Any difficulty level: from simple small ensembles to large-scale productions.
- Copyright notice: We only do non-commercial ensemble practice.
